Code of the Ancestors: How AI is Saving Indigenous Languages

In the mountains of Oaxaca, a quiet revolution is taking place. A team of linguists and software engineers from UNAM has developed a new deep-learning model designed to preserve Zapotec and Mixtec. By training AI on hours of oral history recordings from village elders, the project aims to create the first real-time translation app for Mexico's 68 national languages, ensuring that these ancient voices find a permanent home in the digital future. AI is playing a pivotal role in revitalizing Indigenous languages by enabling communities to create language models, apps, and educational tools.

LIDAR Reveals Lost Cities

New laser scanning technology has stripped away the jungle canopy in Campeche, revealing a sprawling network of Mayan highways and pyramids previously hidden from the human eye. The lidar scans exposed over 6,000 earthen platforms, many serving as ceremonial buildings or homes, arranged around central plazas.

Robots on the Moon: Project Colmena

Mexico's first lunar mission is small but mighty. Five micro-robots, developed by Mexican university students, are set to navigate the lunar surface to study how dust affects electronic equipment. UNAM, successfully launched five micro-robots to the Moon in January 2024 aboard the Astrobotic Peregrine lander.

The 3D-Printed Tortilla

A Jalisco-based startup is reimagining the staple of Mexican cuisine. Using modified 3D food printers, they are creating geometric corn structures that retain nutritional value while offering new textures for haute cuisine. The concept of 3D-printed centers around a satirical tech demonstration from 2013.

Guadalajara: The Silicon Valley of Latin America

While Mexico City is the financial heart, Guadalajara has cemented its reputation as the country's technological brain. With major international chip manufacturers expanding operations and a booming local startup scene focusing on fintech and agri-tech, the state of Jalisco is proving that Mexico is moving beyond manufacturing into high-level innovation and software development.The city’s success is underpinned by a strong innovation ecosystem, a skilled workforce from top universities, and strategic government initiatives aimed at digital and creative development.
